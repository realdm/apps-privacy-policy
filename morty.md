# Privacy Policy

**Morti — Mortgage Calculator**
*Last updated: February 10, 2026*

## The short version

Morti does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no servers. We literally have no way to see what you type into the app.

## Information we collect

None.

Morti is a fully offline calculator. All mortgage calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not collect, process, or have access to:

- Your mortgage details, property values, or financial information
- Personal information such as your name, email, or phone number
- Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
- Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data

There is no account creation, no sign-up, and no login. The app functions entirely without an internet connection.

## Data storage

Any data you enter into the calculator (property values, interest rates, saved scenarios) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to any server or third party.

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

## Third-party services

Morti does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data processing services. There are no SDKs from Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook, or any other data collection platform embedded in the app.

## Advertising

There are no ads in Morti. We do not work with any advertising networks or partners.

## Children's privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, Morti is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, or anyone else.

## Permissions

Morti requests no special device permissions. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, location, or any other device resources.

## Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate material changes to this policy.

## About Morti

Morti is made by the team behind Worthy, a privacy-focused net worth tracker. We believe financial tools should help you without harvesting your data.
